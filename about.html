What I Do

01

Research

I start by listening. Qualitative and quantitative data collection, thematic analysis, and interpretation of it in various artefacts help me understand the real problems people face.

02

Design

Insights become recommended solutions, wireframes, and prototypes if required. I create solutions that balance user needs with technical constraints and business goals.

03

Iterate

Testing reveals what works and what doesn't. I refine designs based on feedback, usability testing, and real world use until the experience feels right.
