Industry Partnership Project with UHN

Beyond   the   Forms

Making a $47M AgeTech innovation program easier to discover, understand, and navigate.

UX Research & Design Lead  ·  8 Months  ·  Team of 2

Improved discoverability and clarity in the envisAGE Agetech funding program by designing a centralized microsite and marketing strategy. It improved clarity around eligibility, steps, and submission requirements by bringing scattered guidance into a single experience.

02

By 2050, 1 in 5 people globally will be over the age of 60. Canada has invested over $150M in age-tech innovation, yet many solutions still fail to reach older adults. While ideas and funding exist, the programs that connect them remain difficult to discover.

The Challenge

AgeTech refers to technologies designed specifically to improve the quality of life, independence, and healthcare access for aging populations.

EnvisAGE is a $47M national Canadian program built to get AgeTech innovations out of the lab and into the hands of older adults. EnvisAGE funds and connects the people building these technologies with the environments needed to test and scale them.

The goal was to understand exactly where and why applicants were getting stuck, and design a way to fix it.

The Target

UHN KITE needed 7 successful SME and Community Lab partnerships within 3 years and was falling short.

The Gap

Most eligible organizations had never heard of the program. Those who knew couldn't navigate it on their own.

03

Understanding the Program Structure

EnvisAGE connects three partners. Small-Medium Enterprises (SMEs) bring the AgeTech innovations. Community Labs (CLs) provide real-world environments to pilot and validate those solutions with older adults. Beachheads sit in the middle, providing research expertise, matching the right SMEs with the right CLs, and guiding everyone.

What Was Found

Across all the sessions, information gaps and low program visibility came up as the most consistent barriers.

4 patterns kept showing up across all sessions ↓
Insight 01

Limited program awareness

Most SMEs and CLs learned about EnvisAGE through internal networks or word of mouth, limiting awareness among potential applicants.

Insight 02

Scattered information

Program details were spread across PDFs, emails, and conversations, requiring applicants to gather information from multiple sources.

Insight 03

Unclear roles

SMEs, CLs, and Beachheads understood their responsibilities differently, causing confusion during applications and collaboration.

Insight 04

Limited early guidance

Applicants lacked proactive guidance at the start of the process and only discovered available support after confusion or errors had already occurred.

07

EnvisAGE was designed to bridge innovation and opportunity.
For most applicants, the bridge itself was hard to find and harder to cross.

Applicants came with strong ideas and real commitment. What they lacked was a reliable map through the roles, the funding structure, and the steps that followed.

08

Project Lighthouse

A centralized information hub supported by a multi-channel outreach strategy.

Part A: Microsite

The site needed to address four key information gaps.

EnvisAGE information was scattered across PDFs, separate websites, and guidance conversations. A microsite within UHN's existing website brings everything into one place.

  • Program structure and role clarity
  • Application guidance with timelines
  • Funding explanations
  • FAQs and success stories

Part B: Awareness Strategy

People cannot apply to a program they've never heard about.

A multi-channel strategy makes the program discoverable beyond conference circuits and closed professional networks.

LinkedIn Content Campaign

12-week content plan aligned with the application cycle, building awareness and driving traffic to the microsite.

Email Onboarding Sequence

Automated and manual emails guiding applicants through the process and reinforcing key deadlines.

SEO Infrastructure

Technical microsite setup for organic discoverability and accessibility compliance.

10

Project Constraints

The project focused on communication, clarity, and experience design surrounding the program — not changes to its core structure.

Application System

Could not modify the EnvisAGE application system or its internal processing flow.

Funding Structure

Funding amounts, reimbursement models, and financial terms were outside scope.

Evaluation Process

Application review criteria and decision-making processes were unchanged.

11

Reflection

EnvisAGE had the funding and the structure to make a real difference. What it was missing was a clear front door.

This project was a reminder that designing for clarity can be just as impactful as designing something new. Access barriers don't always come from broken technology. Sometimes they come from broken communication.
